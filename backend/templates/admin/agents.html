Autonomous AI agents that continuously collect data and generate insights for MediaMap and HealthPIN

What Do These Agents Do?
MediaMap Agent
  • Collects data from media industry RSS feeds
  • Monitors journalism and media news sites
  • Analyzes business models and revenue trends
  • Generates insights about media industry patterns
  • Provides business recommendations
Data Sources: Nieman Lab, Poynter, Journalism.co.uk, MediaPost, O'Reilly Radar
HealthPIN Agent
  • Collects healthcare industry data
  • Monitors medical research and policy updates
  • Analyzes clinical care trends
  • Generates healthcare insights
  • Provides clinical recommendations
Data Sources: Healthcare news feeds, medical journals, policy updates
How it works: Each agent can be started individually to begin running in the background, collecting real data from RSS feeds and websites, analyzing it with AI, and storing insights in the database. The agents learn from patterns and provide recommendations for your business.
